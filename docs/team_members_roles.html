Project Overview
Rimal (رمال) is Jordan's AI Regulatory Sandbox Platform — a structured digital gateway between AI innovators and government regulators. The platform enables companies to submit AI system applications, receive automated risk scoring across 5 compliance dimensions, track review status in real time, and obtain formal regulatory decisions across 8 sectors including Healthcare, Finance, Education, and Public Services. It supports both Arabic and English and integrates with Supabase for secure backend operations.
